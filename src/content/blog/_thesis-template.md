## Summary

Two or three sentences: what the situation was, what I expected, and what happened.

## Situation

What the company does and what had just changed. Keep it to what a reader needs to follow the argument.

## Catalyst

The specific event, its date, and why the market might have mispriced it.

## Reasoning

Why I expected the catalyst to go the way I did, in plain steps. Separate what the evidence shows from what I assumed.

## Risks and what would prove me wrong

What I would have needed to see to exit, and the main ways the thesis could fail (for biotech: trial failure, cash runway, dilution).

## Outcome

What happened, with entry and exit dates. State losses as plainly as gains.

## What I would do differently

One or two honest points on process, not on luck.
